======================================================================
RULING 1 — D05 INHERITS THE I06 P5 CONTRACT CONFLICT
======================================================================

D05 is BLOCKED_CERTIFICATION_CONTRACT under the current certified ownership
doctrine.

Reason:

  A new governed descendant appearing after G7 closure is not in FROZEN_DESC.

  G8 authorized_pid therefore rejects it unless it is otherwise an authorized
  live baseline root.

  To provoke the governed census condition through real pgrep -f, the real
  process must carry a governed-looking signature.

  Certified P5 requires:

    PID_NOT_REAL_GOVERNED_RUNTIME=YES

  A deliberately governed-looking real fixture process conflicts with that
  predicate in the same way already ruled for I06.

No P5 predicate may be waived.

Therefore:

  D05_STATUS=BLOCKED_CERTIFICATION_CONTRACT
  D05_BLOCKING_REASON=P5_NOT_GOVERNED_CONFLICT
  D05_EXPECTED_GATE=G8_SECOND_STACK
  D05_EXECUTION_CREDIT=NONE

This ruling does NOT prove G8_SECOND_STACK unreachable in all possible runtime
states.

It proves the currently authorized fixture model cannot both:
  provoke the required real governed census condition
and
  satisfy certified P5 unchanged.

Do not invent an ownership exception.

======================================================================
RULING 2 — D04 / D06 MAY NOT REPURPOSE SP01
======================================================================

D04 and D06 are BLOCKED_REACHABILITY under the current D-slice authorization.

Accepted source requirement:

  identity is captured at G7 closure
  identity must differ later at teardown revalidation / signal_one

This requires a temporal mutation between:
  G7 freeze
and
  descendant revalidation

The existing SP01 pre-revalidation hook is indeed architecturally adjacent to
that requirement.

However:

  - SP01 remains separately NOT AUTHORIZED under R80U;
  - the hook is byte-frozen to its prior certification purpose;
  - R80AH already established that a frozen hook may not be repurposed as a
    general mutation seam merely because its timing is convenient;
  - D-slice build authorization did not expand SP01 authority.

Therefore the hook may NOT be invoked, rebound, generalized, or treated as an
implicitly authorized D04/D06 mutation mechanism.

D04:

  D04_STATUS=BLOCKED_REACHABILITY
  D04_BLOCKING_REASON=NO_AUTHORIZED_G7_TO_REVALIDATION_IDENTITY_MUTATION_SEAM
  D04_EXPECTED_GATE=TD_IDENTITY_MISMATCH
  D04_EXECUTION_CREDIT=NONE

D06:

  D06_STATUS=BLOCKED_REACHABILITY
  D06_BLOCKING_REASON=NO_AUTHORIZED_G7_TO_REVALIDATION_IDENTITY_MUTATION_SEAM
  D06_EXPECTED_GATE=TD_IDENTITY_MISMATCH
  D06_EXECUTION_CREDIT=NONE

Do not add:
  generic background mutator
  polling mutator
  new revalidation hook
  SP01 rebinding
  race/sleep choreography

without a new Boss ruling.

RULING 4 — D02
======================================================================

D02 remains BUILDABLE IN PRINCIPLE.

Required condition:

  descendant is legitimately captured in FROZEN_DESC before parent disappears

then:

  parent becomes absent
  descendant remains present
  descendant PID/starttime/signature remain unchanged
  frozen provenance continues to authorize that descendant

D02 must not be simulated by merely marking the parent absent in synthetic
PROC_ROOT if the executor's relevant parent-survival/descendant eligibility
logic depends on real process state.

Before encoding D02, CPT must identify exactly:

  which parent-presence observation is real /proc vs PROC_ROOT;
  when parent disappearance must occur;
  whether current authorized fixture cleanup/control can create that state
  without adding a new temporal mutation seam.

If that exact transition can be produced using an already accepted fixture
mechanism:
  D02=BUILT

If it requires a new mid-run mutator:
  D02=BLOCKED_REACHABILITY

Do not assume buildability solely from the conceptual case definition.
