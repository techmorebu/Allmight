REMAINING BLOCKER — D01 IS NOT A ZERO-SIGNAL CASE
======================================================================

v19 currently defines D01 expected/verifier strings as:

  closure=FROZEN
  frozen_contains_desc=YES
  order=CLOSURE_BEFORE_CENSUS
  signals=0
  mutations=0
  starts=0

This is not source-grounded to the R28 D01 obligation.

R28 D01 certifies:

  descendant closure captured before census

D01 is an ordering/provenance case.

It is NOT defined as:

  STOP before signal

and it is not a zero-signal boundary case.

The exact executor may correctly:

  establish G7 closure;
  emit G7_CLOSURE=FROZEN;
  enter the later G8 census;
  continue beyond the point D01 certifies;
  eventually enter teardown / signal-adapter logic.

Under TEST_ADAPTER, a later executor TERM attempt can legitimately increment:

  TERM_ATTEMPTED_COUNT

even though no real signal is delivered.

Therefore requiring:

  signals=0

can falsely FAIL a correct D01 certification run for behavior occurring AFTER
the D01 property has already been demonstrated.

The same principle applies to mutation/start counters unless exact source
analysis establishes they are part of the D01 certification boundary.
