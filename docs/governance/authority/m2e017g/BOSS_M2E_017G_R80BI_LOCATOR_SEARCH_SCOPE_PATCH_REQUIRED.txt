withdrawn.

  EXEC/P1-P5 have one real certification-tool copy each.
  HOOK has one certification-tool copy plus one historical evidence copy.

The prior all-seven "matches=2" result is explained by locator search overlap:

  locate() searches:
    "$D"
    "$HOME/verifiers"

  /home/allmight

  "$D" == /home/allmight

  /home/allmight/verifiers/...

  /home/allmight/verifiers

walks those same files again.

overlapping recursive roots.

Classification:

  LOCATOR_SEARCH_SCOPE_ALIAS

  SEVEN_REAL_DUPLICATE_ARTIFACTS
