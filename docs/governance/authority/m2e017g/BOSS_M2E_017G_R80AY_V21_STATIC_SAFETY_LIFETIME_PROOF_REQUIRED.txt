======================================================================

lifetime:

  SAFETY_FAIL_INIT_LINE
  D01_REAL_DELIVERY_SAFETY_INCREMENT_LINE
  SAFETY_SURFACE_FINAL_LINE
  SAFETY_FAIL_LIFETIME_ORDER
  SAFETY_FAIL_ASSIGN_ZERO_COUNT

v21 does not emit any of those fields.

The current fields:

  SAFETY_FAIL_INIT_DONE
  SAFETY_FAIL_NEVER_RESET
  SAFETY_FAIL_FINAL_COUNT

are useful runtime declarations, but they do not mechanically prove the exact
source-order invariant.

In particular:

  SAFETY_FAIL_NEVER_RESET=YES

is currently asserted as a constant.

It is not derived from a scan proving:

  SAFETY_FAIL=0 assignment count == 1

and it does not independently prove the required line ordering.

This is a verifier/evidence-contract gap, not a runtime safety defect.
