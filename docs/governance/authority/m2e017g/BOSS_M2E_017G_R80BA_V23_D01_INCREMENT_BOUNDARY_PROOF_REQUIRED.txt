REMAINING DEFECT — D01 INCREMENT SEARCH IS NOT ACTUALLY BOUNDED
======================================================================

v23 locates the D01 increment with:

  SF_D01_INC_LINE=$(awk -v st="$SF_D01_ANCHOR_LINE" \
    'NR>=st && /^[[:space:]]*SAFETY_FAIL=\$\(\(SAFETY_FAIL\+1\)\).../{print NR; exit}' "$SELF_SRC")

This starts at the unique D01 anchor, but it has NO upper boundary.

Therefore, if the real D01 increment at line 992 were accidentally removed,
the probe would continue scanning the rest of the runner and could select the
next unrelated SAFETY_FAIL increment in the later safety-surface code.

The proof could then still satisfy:

  init_line < "D01 increment" < final_line

even though the D01 real-delivery branch no longer increments SAFETY_FAIL.

That is another verifier false-positive path.

Current exact bytes happen to resolve correctly to line 992, but the verifier
must prove the structural relationship, not merely succeed on today's layout.
