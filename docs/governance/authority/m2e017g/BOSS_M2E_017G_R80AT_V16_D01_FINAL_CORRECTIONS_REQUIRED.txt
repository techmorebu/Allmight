======================================================================
BLOCKER 4 — REVALIDATED-KILL PROBE CANNOT FIRE
======================================================================

v16 currently computes:

  RK_EXACT=$(grep -cE '${CMDPOS}kill -TERM "\$_q"' "$SCAN_SRC" || true)

The pattern is single-quoted.

Therefore `${CMDPOS}` is passed literally to grep and is NOT expanded.

This is a BUG-009 verifier-precision defect.

The probe can falsely report zero even though the direct TERM site exists.

Worse, the resulting:

  RUNNER_KILL_IS_REVALIDATED_FIXTURE_PID_ONLY

is descriptive only; a NO result does not increment SAFETY_FAIL.

REQUIRED CORRECTION:

Use the same measured code-only source universe as the primary kill gate.

Construct the exact-site probe so CMDPOS is actually applied, or use a
fixed-string/source-position method that cannot be broken by shell/ERE
expansion.

Known positive:
  the one authorized D01 kill -TERM "$_q" site must be found exactly once.

Required:

  D01_EXACT_REVALIDATED_KILL_SITE_COUNT=1
  RUNNER_KILL_IS_REVALIDATED_FIXTURE_PID_ONLY=YES

and this MUST be a gating safety predicate:

  if count != 1:
    SAFETY_FAIL++

The safety surface must therefore require:

  REAL_KILL_CALL_SITES=1
  D01_EXACT_REVALIDATED_KILL_SITE_COUNT=1
  RUNNER_PATTERN_KILL_SITES=0
  SIGNAL_DISCLOSURE_EQ_GATE=YES

Do not allow an advisory NO to coexist with SAFETY_SURFACE=PASS.
