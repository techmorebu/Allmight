PROJECT: ALLMIGHT
PHASE: 7 — LIVE EXECUTION (GUARDED)

AUTHORITATIVE CONTRACT (v0)

Phase-7 responsibilities:
- Consume Phase-6 execution plans as the sole authority
- Enforce plan status, allowed modes, execution_policy, and gating_chain
- Require explicit operator arming for any side-effect-capable execution
- Produce deterministic execution receipts and audit traces
- Preserve idempotency and temporal integrity (asof isolation)

Phase-7 prohibitions:
- Must NOT derive intent, scores, bands, confidence, or suppression
- Must NOT rebuild or modify execution plans
- Must NOT execute suppressed or disallowed plans
- Must NOT access network by default
- Must NOT execute without explicit adapter selection

This contract is immutable unless superseded by a later signed Phase document.
