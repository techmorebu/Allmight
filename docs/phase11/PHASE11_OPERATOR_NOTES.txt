1) WHAT PHASE 11 ADDS (OPERATOR VIEW)
------------------------------------
Phase 11 adds *read-only* snapshot reconciliation building blocks:

A) Merge policies (allmight/adapters/snapshot_merge.py)
- pick_first:
  deterministic, first snapshot wins.
- pick_first_valid:
  deterministic, skips invalid (<=0, NaN/inf), first valid wins;
  refuses with REFUSE_FIRST_VALID_NOT_FOUND if none valid.
- median:
  median price of valid snapshots; even counts average two center values.
- reject_outliers:
  rejects far-out prices and merges remaining “center mass”;
  refuses with REFUSE_ALL_REJECTED when nothing remains.
- median_strict:
  requires min_sources (after invalid filtering) AND spread <= max_spread_pct.
  refusals:
    - REFUSE_MEDIAN_STRICT_TOO_FEW_SOURCES
    - REFUSE_MEDIAN_STRICT_SPREAD

B) Normalization helper (allmight/adapters/snapshot_normalize_merge.py)
- normalize_and_merge:
  - enforces pair consistency (refuses mismatched pair sets)
  - filters invalid prices
  - delegates to merge policy

C) Aggregate result (allmight/adapters/snapshot_aggregate.py)
- SnapshotAggregate:
  - merged snapshot
  - audit fields to explain what got kept/rejected and why

D) Band helper (allmight/adapters/snapshot_band.py)
- small pure utility to compute spread/band decisions for strict reconciliation
