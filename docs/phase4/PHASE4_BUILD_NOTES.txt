PHASE: 4
TITLE: Phase 4 Build Notes — Execution Enablement (Controlled / Capital-Aware)
STATUS: ACTIVE (Build Complete to Current Scope)

------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE CONTEXT
------------------------------------------------------------

Phase 4 follows the successful completion and freeze of Phase 3,
which established execution preparation under strict denial-first,
non-capital conditions.

Phase 4 exists to introduce the *concept and structure* of
capital-backed execution while preserving all Phase 3 safety,
permission, and audit guarantees.

------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE OBJECTIVE
------------------------------------------------------------

Enable controlled execution capability by:

- Defining execution adapters capable of live operation
- Explicitly declaring capital existence and scope
- Extending safety systems to capital-aware risk classes
- Validating that live-capable concepts remain bounded, gated,
  and non-operational by default

Phase 4 does NOT exist to trade profitably or scale execution.

------------------------------------------------------------
KEY OUTCOMES
------------------------------------------------------------

- Execution adapters are explicitly typed (PAPER / SHADOW / LIVE)
- Capital exposure is declared, bounded, isolated, and revocable
- No implicit capital access exists anywhere in the system
- Permission gates remain denial-first and authoritative
- Halt and kill-switch semantics extend to capital-aware risks
- Capital-specific failure modes are enumerated and mitigated
- Repository state remains compliant with Phase-0 governance
- No live execution is enabled by default

------------------------------------------------------------
CONFIRMED NON-GOALS
------------------------------------------------------------

The following were intentionally NOT performed in Phase 4:

- Production trading
- Capital scaling
- Strategy optimization
- Weight or regime changes
- Performance tuning
- Automated capital recovery
- Removal or softening of safety constraints

------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE SAFETY ASSESSMENT
------------------------------------------------------------

Phase 4 introduces *declared risk without operational exposure*.

All capital-aware concepts:
- Are explicitly documented
- Are permission-gated
- Are halt- and kill-enforced
- Remain non-operational unless explicitly activated

No capital loss is possible under current Phase 4 state.

------------------------------------------------------------
DETERMINISM & AUDITABILITY
------------------------------------------------------------

- All execution concepts are explainable without chat context
- All safety behavior is explicit and documented
- No nondeterministic shortcuts were introduced
- Phase Scan confirms no undeclared execution paths exist
