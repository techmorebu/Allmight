PURPOSE
-------
This appendix defines the complete lifecycle for introducing non-generative,
latent intelligence models into AllMight.

It unifies:
- Latent (non-generative) reasoning architecture
- JEPA-style market world modeling
- Shadow-mode A/B testing
- Evaluation metrics
- Activation and rollback criteria

No component described here may be activated without passing the gates
defined in this document.

============================================================
SECTION 1 — CORE PHILOSOPHY
============================================================

AllMight separates intelligence into layers:

- Cognition: silent, latent, non-generative
- Language: optional translation layer
- Execution: guarded, deterministic, auditable

Language is not intelligence.
Tokens are not reasoning.
Markets are dynamic systems, not text streams.

Latent models operate in semantic space, maintaining continuous internal state.
Language models serialize meaning only when required.

============================================================
SECTION 2 — LATENT INTELLIGENCE MODULE
============================================================

MODULE NAME:
LatentEngineCore

ROLE:
- Maintain continuous internal market understanding
- Track temporal dynamics and causality
- Output embeddings, confidence, regime, and action readiness
- Never generate text by default

INPUTS:
- Market data (price, volume, volatility)
- Cross-asset relationships
- Macro indicators
- Execution feedback
- Time context

OUTPUTS (NON-LINGUISTIC):
- Regime embedding
- Confidence score
- Risk gradient
- Action readiness flag
- Stability indicator

CONSTRAINTS:
- Python-first
- GPU-accelerated where available
- Deterministic updates
- Tile-compatible operations preferred
- Fully auditable state transitions

============================================================
SECTION 3 — JEPA-STYLE MARKET WORLD MODEL
============================================================

Markets are treated as a physical system with hidden state.

ARCHITECTURE:

X-Encoder (Observations):
- Market microstructure
- Time-series features
- Volatility and flow
- Macro context

Latent Predictor:
- Predicts next latent state
- No price targets
- No token prediction

Y-Encoder (Optional Queries):
- Risk
- Trend
- Instability
- Exposure

LOSS:
- Alignment between predicted and observed latent states
- Penalizes incoherent temporal jumps

KEY PROPERTY:
Meaning stabilizes over time.
Instant guesses may be wrong.
Locked meaning is prioritized.

============================================================

============================================================

GOAL:

without granting execution authority.

Baseline (A):

============================================================
SECTION 5 — EVALUATION METRICS (MINIMAL SET)
============================================================

PRIMARY:
- Max Drawdown (absolute + relative)
- Tail loss (worst 1% days)
- Profit Factor
- Sharpe or Sortino

DECISION QUALITY:
- Trade precision
- False-positive rate (bad trades)
- Regime churn index
- Missed major regime shifts (secondary)

TEMPORAL COHERENCE:
- Time to lock stable regime
- Revision rate after lock
- Output variance during noise

OPERATIONAL:
- Latency delta
- GPU/CPU/RAM usage
- Failure rate

============================================================
SECTION 6 — ACTIVATION GATES
============================================================

ALL must be true before activation:

DATA & HARNESS:
- Data pipelines stable ≥ 14 days
- Deterministic replay confirmed
- Shadow harness runs ≥ 7 days without error

PERFORMANCE (must hit ≥ 2):
- Drawdown reduced ≥ 10% relative
- Tail loss improved ≥ 15%
- Regime churn reduced ≥ 20%
- Profit quality improved without higher risk

OPERATIONS:
- Latency impact < 5% (hard cap 10%)
- No crash loops
- Outputs interpretable by humans

============================================================
SECTION 7 — ACTIVATION PHASES
============================================================

Phase 1: Shadow Mode
- Log-only
- No execution authority

Phase 2: Advisory Mode
- Recommendations visible
- Still no execution

Phase 3: Limited Authority
- Candidate may veto trades
- Cannot initiate trades

Phase 4: Full Authority
- Candidate may propose actions
- Guardrails always enforced

============================================================
SECTION 8 — ROLLBACK RULES
============================================================

Immediate rollback if:
- Drawdown worsens > 10% vs baseline
- Tail loss worsens > 15%
- Latency increases > 10% sustained
- Nondeterminism detected
- Unexplained divergence persists > 48h

Rollback action:
- Disable candidate authority
- Revert to baseline
- Preserve logs for forensics
