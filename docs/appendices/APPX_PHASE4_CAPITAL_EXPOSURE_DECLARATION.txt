APPENDIX ID: APPX-PHASE4-CAPITAL-DECL-01
TITLE: Phase 4 Capital Exposure Declaration
STATUS: CANONICAL
APPLIES TO: Phase 4 (Execution Enablement)

------------------------------------------------------------
PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------

This appendix explicitly declares the existence and scope of
capital exposure permitted during Phase 4.

Its purpose is to ensure that:
- Capital exposure is intentional and bounded
- Capital is isolated to explicit execution adapters
- Capital can be halted or withdrawn immediately
- No subsystem may assume capital availability implicitly

If capital is not declared here, it does not exist.

------------------------------------------------------------
CORE PRINCIPLE
------------------------------------------------------------

CAPITAL IS A CONTROLLED LIABILITY.

Capital exposure is permitted only as explicitly declared,
never inferred from configuration or code presence.

------------------------------------------------------------
DECLARATION OF CAPITAL EXISTENCE
------------------------------------------------------------

Phase 4 acknowledges the existence of REAL capital.

This capital is introduced solely for the purpose of:
- Validating live execution mechanics
- Verifying end-to-end execution safety
- Observing real-world execution behavior

Capital introduction does NOT imply profit-seeking,
scale, or production readiness.

------------------------------------------------------------
CAPITAL SCOPE (BOUNDARY)
------------------------------------------------------------

All Phase 4 capital exposure is subject to the following bounds:

- Capital is limited to a predefined maximum amount
- Capital is segregated from all other assets
- Capital is non-essential and sacrificial
- Loss of all declared capital MUST NOT endanger the project

No capital outside this scope may be accessed.

------------------------------------------------------------
CAPITAL LOCATION & ISOLATION
------------------------------------------------------------

Declared capital MUST:

- Reside in explicitly designated accounts or wallets
- Be isolated per execution adapter
- Be inaccessible to:
  - Strategy logic
  - Regime logic
  - Permission logic
  - Any non-execution subsystem

Capital isolation is mandatory and non-negotiable.

------------------------------------------------------------
AUTHORIZED CAPITAL ACCESS
------------------------------------------------------------

Capital may be accessed ONLY by:

- Execution adapters explicitly typed as LIVE
- Adapters that conform to APPX-PHASE4-EXEC-ADAPTER-01
- Adapters operating under explicit execution permission

No other component may access capital under any condition.

------------------------------------------------------------
CAPITAL ACCESS MODES
------------------------------------------------------------

Capital access is restricted to:

- Order placement
- Order cancellation
- Balance inspection (read-only)

The following are FORBIDDEN:

- Capital transfer between accounts
- Capital withdrawal without operator action
- Capital reuse outside declared adapter scope
- Automated capital scaling

------------------------------------------------------------
CAPITAL EXPOSURE LIMITS
------------------------------------------------------------

For Phase 4:

- Maximum capital at risk is FIXED and declared externally
- Capital limits are enforced at adapter initialization
- Breach or attempted breach triggers a kill switch

Capital limits MUST be auditable and verifiable.

------------------------------------------------------------
HALT & KILL CONDITIONS (CAPITAL-SPECIFIC)
------------------------------------------------------------

The following conditions trigger an IMMEDIATE KILL SWITCH:

- Unauthorized capital access attempt
- Access outside declared adapter scope
- Exceeding declared capital limits
- Loss of connectivity to capital control systems
- Detection of implicit or leaked capital access

On kill:
- All execution stops
- Capital access is frozen
- No automatic recovery is permitted

------------------------------------------------------------
WITHDRAWAL & RECOVERY RULES
------------------------------------------------------------

Capital withdrawal:

- Requires explicit operator action
- Occurs outside automated execution flows
- Is logged and auditable

Automated capital recovery is FORBIDDEN.

------------------------------------------------------------
AUDIT & TRACEABILITY
------------------------------------------------------------

All capital interactions MUST be logged with:

- Adapter identity
- Capital scope identifier
- Action taken
- Permission state
- Halt / kill state
- Timestamp

Logs MUST allow reconstruction of all capital exposure
without chat context.

------------------------------------------------------------
PROHIBITED ASSUMPTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

The following assumptions are INVALID:

- Capital exists because code can trade
- Capital may be reused across adapters
- Capital access is safe because limits exist
- Capital can be scaled automatically
- Capital risk is acceptable without documentation

------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE BOUNDARY GUARANTEE
------------------------------------------------------------

Phase 4 capital exposure:

- Is limited, explicit, and revocable
- Does not imply production readiness
- Does not authorize strategy changes
- Does not weaken Phase 3 safety guarantees

------------------------------------------------------------
STATUS
------------------------------------------------------------

This declaration is binding for Phase 4.
Any capital exposure outside this declaration is INVALID.
