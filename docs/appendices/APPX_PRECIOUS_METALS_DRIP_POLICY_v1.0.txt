PRECIOUS METALS DRIP ALLOCATION POLICY
Version: v1.0
Scope: Income, Stability, Compounding
============================================================

1. POLICY OBJECTIVE
------------------------------------------------------------
This policy defines how precious metals exposure is used to:
- Generate repeatable income
- Support DRIP compounding mechanics
- Preserve capital during macro stress
- Avoid leverage, margin, and derivative fragility

Precious metals are treated as:
- Income-supporting assets (via businesses)
- Volatility dampeners
- Inflation and regime hedges

Raw metal price speculation is explicitly excluded.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. APPROVED ALLOCATION TIERS
------------------------------------------------------------

TOTAL PRECIOUS METALS ALLOCATION: 100%
(Capped at a system-defined percentage of total portfolio)

TIER A — ROYALTY & STREAMING COMPANIES (50–65%)
------------------------------------------------
ROLE:
- Core DRIP income engine
- Lowest operational risk metal exposure
- Dividend-first mandate

CHARACTERISTICS:
- Fixed-cost access to metal production
- No direct mining operations
- High margins across price regimes
- Strong free cash flow visibility

DRIP RULES:
- Dividends auto-reinvested while weekly income < milestone
- Eligible for partial payout only after DRIP thresholds are met

------------------------------------------------------------
TIER B — PHYSICAL METALS (30–40%)
------------------------------------------------
ROLE:
- Capital preservation
- System insurance
- Non-correlated reserve

ALLOCATION GUIDANCE:
- Gold: 60–70% of Tier B
- Silver: 30–40% of Tier B

ACQUISITION:
- Physical bullion only
- Government-issued coins or high-liquidity bars
- Non-bank storage preferred

YIELD EXPECTATION:
- None required
- Leasing or yield overlays optional but capped

DRIP RULES:
- No forced selling for income
- Can be pledged only to fund upgrades or emergency stabilization

------------------------------------------------------------
TIER C — DIVIDEND-PAYING TIER-1 MINERS (5–15%)
------------------------------------------------
ROLE:
- Supplemental income
- Controlled growth exposure

QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS:
- Low AISC (All-In Sustaining Cost)
- Strong balance sheet
- Established dividend policy
- Multi-jurisdiction operations

RESTRICTIONS:
- No junior miners
- No single-asset producers
- No exploration-only exposure

------------------------------------------------------------
3. DRIP INTEGRATION RULES
------------------------------------------------------------

- All dividends flow into DRIP Vault
- Weekly payout eligibility follows global DRIP ladder:
  $300 → $500 → $800 → $1300 → … → $10,000 cap
- If weekly income falls below a threshold:
  → Revert to prior level
  → Full reinvestment resumes automatically

Precious metals income is prioritized for:
1) Capital stability
2) Baseline income reliability
3) Long-term compounding

------------------------------------------------------------
4. RISK CONTROLS & EXCLUSIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

EXPLICITLY EXCLUDED:
- Futures
- Margin trading
- Leveraged ETFs
- Paper-only metal derivatives
- “Squeeze” narratives

KILL CONDITIONS:
- Dividend suspension across Tier A majority
- Jurisdictional confiscation risk escalation
- Counterparty failure in custody chain

------------------------------------------------------------
5. SYSTEM VERDICT
------------------------------------------------------------

Precious metals support DRIP ONLY when wrapped in:
- Cash-flowing business models
- Contractual royalty structures
- Conservative dividend policies

This policy is designed to survive:
- High inflation
- Monetary regime transitions
- Equity volatility
- Liquidity contractions
