APPENDIX ID: APPX-PHASE4-RISK-MITIGATION-01
TITLE: Phase 4 Risk Extension & Mitigation Specification
STATUS: CANONICAL
APPLIES TO: Phase 4 (Execution Enablement — Capital Aware)

------------------------------------------------------------
PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------

This appendix defines the additional risk classes introduced
by capital-backed execution in Phase 4 and the mandatory
mitigation, halt, and kill responses for each.

Its purpose is to ensure that:
- Capital-specific risks are explicitly acknowledged
- Unsafe market or system behavior halts execution immediately
- Loss modes are bounded, observable, and reversible
- Phase 3 safety guarantees remain intact under live conditions

------------------------------------------------------------
CORE PRINCIPLE
------------------------------------------------------------

KNOWN RISK IS MANAGEABLE.
UNENUMERATED RISK IS UNACCEPTABLE.

Any risk not explicitly addressed here MUST be treated
as a kill condition until formally documented.

------------------------------------------------------------
RISK TAXONOMY (PHASE 4)
------------------------------------------------------------

Phase 4 introduces the following new risk classes:

1. Market Execution Risk
2. Liquidity & Slippage Risk
3. Partial Fill & Order State Risk
4. Exchange / Venue Availability Risk
5. Network & Connectivity Risk
6. Capital Accounting Risk
7. Key & Credential Exposure Risk
8. State Desynchronization Risk

Each class is addressed below.

------------------------------------------------------------
1. MARKET EXECUTION RISK
------------------------------------------------------------

Description:
- Orders may execute at prices different from intent
- Market conditions may change mid-execution

Mitigations:
- All orders MUST be price-bounded
- Market orders are FORBIDDEN unless explicitly approved
- Execution intent MUST include acceptable price ranges

Halt Conditions:
- Detected execution outside declared bounds

Kill Conditions:
- Repeated price violations
- Adapter bypasses price constraints

------------------------------------------------------------
2. LIQUIDITY & SLIPPAGE RISK
------------------------------------------------------------

Description:
- Insufficient liquidity may cause excessive slippage

Mitigations:
- Slippage limits MUST be declared per adapter
- Orders exceeding slippage bounds MUST be rejected

Halt Conditions:
- Slippage exceeds declared threshold

Kill Conditions:
- Adapter ignores slippage constraints
- Slippage cannot be reliably measured

------------------------------------------------------------
3. PARTIAL FILL & ORDER STATE RISK
------------------------------------------------------------

Description:
- Orders may partially fill or remain open

Mitigations:
- Partial fills MUST be explicitly handled
- Open orders MUST be tracked deterministically
- Orphaned orders MUST NOT persist silently

Halt Conditions:
- Unknown or ambiguous order state

Kill Conditions:
- Loss of order state reconciliation
- Duplicate or phantom orders detected

------------------------------------------------------------
4. EXCHANGE / VENUE AVAILABILITY RISK
------------------------------------------------------------

Description:
- Exchanges may go offline, throttle, or reject requests

Mitigations:
- Execution adapters MUST handle explicit rejection
- Retry logic MUST be bounded and conservative

Halt Conditions:
- Temporary venue unavailability
- Rate limit or throttle events

Kill Conditions:
- Venue behavior becomes undefined
- Inconsistent or corrupt responses detected

------------------------------------------------------------
5. NETWORK & CONNECTIVITY RISK
------------------------------------------------------------

Description:
- Network partitions or latency may disrupt execution

Mitigations:
- Timeouts MUST be explicit and short
- Execution MUST fail closed on connectivity loss

Halt Conditions:
- Transient connectivity loss

Kill Conditions:
- Prolonged or unstable connectivity
- Split-brain execution risk detected

------------------------------------------------------------
6. CAPITAL ACCOUNTING RISK
------------------------------------------------------------

Description:
- Reported balances may be stale or inconsistent

Mitigations:
- Balance checks MUST be read-only
- Capital limits MUST be enforced locally

Halt Conditions:
- Balance data unavailable or stale

Kill Conditions:
- Inconsistent capital accounting
- Capital usage exceeds declared bounds

------------------------------------------------------------
7. KEY & CREDENTIAL EXPOSURE RISK
------------------------------------------------------------

Description:
- Keys or credentials may be leaked or misused

Mitigations:
- Keys MUST be isolated per adapter
- No key reuse across environments
- No keys accessible outside execution context

Halt Conditions:
- Suspected credential misuse

Kill Conditions:
- Confirmed credential exposure
- Unauthorized signing attempt

------------------------------------------------------------
8. STATE DESYNCHRONIZATION RISK
------------------------------------------------------------

Description:
- Internal state may diverge from external reality

Mitigations:
- Execution state MUST be reconciled explicitly
- No assumptions of atomicity across systems

Halt Conditions:
- State reconciliation uncertainty

Kill Conditions:
- Irrecoverable state divergence
- Execution behavior becomes non-auditable

------------------------------------------------------------
GLOBAL MITIGATION RULES
------------------------------------------------------------

Across ALL risk classes:

- Halt is preferred over continuation
- Kill is preferred over ambiguity
- No automatic recovery from kill
- Operator intervention is required for reset

------------------------------------------------------------
AUDIT & LOGGING REQUIREMENTS
------------------------------------------------------------

All risk events MUST log:

- Risk class
- Trigger condition
- Adapter identity
- Capital scope
- Action taken (halt or kill)
- Timestamp

Logs MUST support forensic reconstruction.

------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE BOUNDARY GUARANTEE
------------------------------------------------------------

This appendix:

- Extends Phase 3 safety into capital-aware execution
- Does NOT authorize new execution capability
- Does NOT permit risk-taking beyond declared bounds
- Does NOT imply production readiness

------------------------------------------------------------
STATUS
------------------------------------------------------------

This specification is binding for Phase 4.
Any capital-aware execution that violates this appendix
is INVALID.
