------------------------------------------------------------
1. MODULE PURPOSE (BOUNDARY LOCK)
------------------------------------------------------------

This module exists only to:
- Detect market stress and liquidity fragility
- Identify forced deleveraging risk
- Adjust execution risk posture and sizing constraints
- Support long-horizon capital preservation bias

This module explicitly does NOT:
- Predict prices
- Generate trade entries or exits
- Assert manipulation or intent
- Override regime detection
- Influence DRIP payout thresholds or compounding math

2. PROXY MATRIX (FINAL)
------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 Margin & Leverage Shock
Mechanism:
- Exchange margin changes mechanically force deleveraging → volatility expansion

Proxies:
- Margin requirement changes (CME / COMEX)
- Open interest delta (±72h)
- Intraday ATR expansion (Z-score)
- Forced liquidation clusters

System Effect:
- Elevate Execution Risk State
- Reduce allowable leverage
- Widen safety buffers

- Crowded positioning increases fragility, not certainty

Proxies:

- Managed money crowding metrics
- Rate-of-change in net exposure

System Effect:

- Volatility expectation adjustment

2.3 Paper vs Physical Divergence
Mechanism:
- Delivery stress manifests as premium and inventory divergence

Proxies:

- ETF flows (secondary sentiment proxy only)

System Effect:

2.4 Structural Supply–Demand Constraint
Mechanism:
- Demand acceleration + supply rigidity → tail risk skew

Proxies:
- Annual supply–demand balance
- Mine output trend
- Recycling contribution trend
- Base-metal production (byproduct proxy)

System Effect:
- Strategic allocation justification only
- No timing or execution authority

2.5 Relative Valuation Context
Mechanism:
- Ratios provide regime context, not signals

Proxies:
- Gold–silver ratio percentile bands
- Correlation stability vs regime layer

System Effect:
- Rebalancing context only
