SECTION 3 — EXECUTION MODEL
------------------------------------------------------------
Primary mechanism: Flash loans (Aave V3 on Arbitrum/Base/Optimism)

Flash loan model properties:
- Zero principal at risk per trade
- Profit = spread captured minus fees minus gas
- Capital exposure = fee reserves + slippage buffer only
- Atomic execution -- trade succeeds or reverts with no loss

Capital rules (fee reserve and buffer management):
- Per-trade fee buffer: 2-5% of expected profit
  NOTE: Future revision will use max(fixed_floor_USD, volatility_factor * expected_profit)
  to prevent underbuffering when profit is small but gas is spiky
- Max concurrent trades: 3
- Daily drawdown limit on reserves: 5-8%
- Hard halt threshold: 12%

Compounding model:
- 60-75% reinvest to fee reserve
- 15-25% reserve (liquidity buffer)
- 5-15% hedge allocation

SECTION 4 — MAINNET VIABILITY GATE (MVI)
------------------------------------------------------------
Mainnet is DISABLED by default.

Activation requires passing at least 4 of 5:
1. 14-day average spread persistence above defined threshold
2. Gas volatility below defined ceiling
3. Fee reserve above viability threshold
4. Shadow-mode win rate above 60%
5. Shadow-mode drawdown below 5%

Activation phases:
  DISABLED -> SHADOW -> LIMITED (max 10% reserve) -> ENABLED

No full allocation without shadow validation.
Shadow mode = full simulation with real market data, no on-chain execution.

------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 5 — COMPLEXITY GOVERNANCE RULE
------------------------------------------------------------
A new execution module must satisfy at least 2 of 4:
1. Increases measurable edge (bps or frequency)
2. Improves capital efficiency
3. Reduces variance
4. Preserves determinism (same inputs, same outputs)

If not, reject.
