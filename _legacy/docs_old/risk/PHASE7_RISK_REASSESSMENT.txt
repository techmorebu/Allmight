
PROJECT ALLMIGHT — PHASE 7 RISK REASSESSMENT
===========================================

As-Of (UTC): 2026-01-20T11:59:53Z

Risk Changes Introduced
-----------------------
- Arming TTL enforcement reduces risk of stale/forgotten arming states.

Residual Risks
--------------
- Operator workflows that modify policy/config must restore canonical values
  before wrapping/committing, to preserve clean, auditable history.

Mitigations
-----------
- Prefer policy enforcement driven by committed config.
- Prefer operator drills that write temporary files, or ensure restore step.
